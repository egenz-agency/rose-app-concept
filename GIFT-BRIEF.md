# Project Rose — The Enchanted Rose
### A love story told through a single flower

*(A creative brief written to be handed to an AI so it can generate video prompts for social media. Read it for the feeling, not the mechanics.)*

---

## The heart of it

A man built an entire world for one woman.

Not an app for millions. A gift for **one person** — his girlfriend, Stella. It lives on a screen, but it beats like something alive. When she opens it, she finds a single glowing rose floating in the dark, sealed under a glass dome, turning slowly. Stars drift around it. And every day, she comes back to tend it.

That's the whole thing. A rose she keeps alive with her love. A world that grows richer every time she returns, and quietly dies if she forgets.

It was made to feel like it was made for exactly one person — **because it was.**

---

## Inspired by Beauty and the Beast

The rose is *that* rose — the enchanted rose from Beauty and the Beast, suspended in glass, glowing, its petals counting down the time left for love to prove itself. The very first time she opens the experience, the enchanted-rose scene from the film plays, and then her own rose appears in the dark.

But this story flips the fairytale. In the film, falling petals meant a curse and a deadline. Here, the petals are a promise: as long as she keeps showing up, the rose blooms brighter, and a whole enchanted world blossoms around it. The Beast waited, afraid, for someone to choose him. This rose blooms because someone *does* — every single day.

It's a love story like no other, because it isn't told in words. It's told in a flower she has to choose to keep alive.

---

## The gift, and what it means

This is a love letter that never ends — one that keeps writing itself as long as she keeps coming back.

- **Tending the rose is showing up for someone you love.** Press and hold, and the rose spins and blooms, sparks of light swirl outward, and a new star is born into the sky around it. Every visit is a small act of devotion, and the world remembers it.
- **Neglect has a cost.** Miss a day and a petal falls. Miss too many and the rose fades. He gave it the power to die on purpose — because a love you can't lose isn't a love you'd fight for. There's a way to revive it, to hold a single dying ember and breathe it back to life. But even that runs out one day.
- **Time becomes the romance.** The longer she tends it, the more the darkness fills with life. A garden grows around the dome. Butterflies arrive. Stars multiply — each one a memory they made. A greenhouse begins to glow. What starts as one flower in the void becomes an entire enchanted garden, grown out of nothing but her faithfulness.
- **Letters wait for her.** Hidden love letters unlock as the days add up — at a week, a month, a hundred days, a year. The last one is only reached by someone who stayed the whole way. "One Year — The Last Letter."
- **He can keep surprising her, secretly.** Behind the scenes, he can slip new messages, photos, little video moments, and even date invitations into her world — surfacing on a certain day, or the exact right morning. She'll be tending her rose and suddenly find a note that says *he was here too.* The gift keeps giving long after it was given.

Every word in it is written like a letter. "She needs you." "The rose remembers you." "A single ember still burns — hold it, and breathe her back to life." Nothing about it is casual. It's tender the whole way through.

---

## What it actually is (so you picture it right)

This is a real, working **web application** — a site she opens on her phone or laptop, and can even install to her home screen like an app ("Stella's Rose"). It opens full-screen into darkness, and everything happens in one living scene.

At the center is a genuine **3D model of a rose** — not a picture, an actual rotating, blooming, three-dimensional flower rendered in real time inside a glass dome. She can move the camera around it: drag to orbit it 360°, zoom in close enough to see the petals and leaves, or pull back until it's a tiny point of light in a sky full of stars. It slowly turns on its own, tilts gently toward her cursor, and reacts to everything she does. The dome, the glow, the stars, the falling petals, the garden that grows around it — all of it is a real-time 3D world she moves through, not a video.

So when you imagine footage: think **screen recordings of a live 3D scene** — a red rose glowing under glass in the dark, camera gliding around it, petals drifting, stars orbiting, light blooming when she interacts.

---

## The functionalities (what she can actually do)

- **Open it and be pulled into the story.** First visit: a loading screen "awakens the rose," the Beauty and the Beast enchanted-rose clip plays, the camera sweeps down out of the dark, and the rose blooms open for the first time with cinematic lines of text.
- **Tend the rose every day.** The core ritual. She returns and cares for it — the app tracks her daily visits, her streak of consecutive days, and how faithful she's been. A small heart-and-number streak badge floats above the rose.
- **Press and hold the dome — the "magic" moment.** Holding the glass fills a golden ring; when it completes, the camera rushes in, the rose spins and blooms, hundreds of glowing sparks swirl out, and **a new memory star is born** into the constellation around the rose. Tap instead of hold, and the glass dome lifts to reveal the rose underneath.
- **Watch a whole world grow from her devotion.** Petals regrow as she tends it and fall when she's away. Over weeks and months a garden, butterflies, a flower ring, and a glowing greenhouse appear in stages — the scene visibly gets more alive the longer she keeps coming.
- **Collect a sky of memories.** Every magic moment adds a star; the stars orbit the rose, connected by faint golden lines, forming a personal constellation she builds over time. She can open a panel to see and manage them.
- **Read letters as they unlock.** Hidden love letters open at milestones — 7 days, 30, 100, 365 — each one a longer, deeper note. They stay locked until she's earned them by returning.
- **Get daily notes and a growing fairytale.** Each visit shows a message — sometimes a random love note, sometimes the next "chapter" of an ongoing fairytale that advances every few days as the rose's story unfolds.
- **Revive it if it dies.** If she neglects it too long the rose fades — but she can hold a dying ember to breathe it back to life. She has a limited number of revivals; if they run out, a final cinematic scene and a last letter play. The stakes are real.
- **Change her view.** Quick buttons to jump the camera: *Near* (close on the bloom), *Dome* (full view), *Heavens* (pulled back into the stars).
- **Discover his surprises.** Behind a private, password-protected page, he can schedule things into her world: custom messages that override the daily note, photo and video "moments" that appear on a chosen day or visit, and **interactive date invitations** she can open like an envelope and answer — accept his proposed time, or suggest another, and her reply comes back to him.

---

## The feeling to capture

Slow. Warm. Aching in the good way. Candlelight in the dark. A single red rose glowing under glass while the whole world holds its breath around it. Gold light, deep crimson, near-black velvet dark, drifting stars. Every movement is soft and weighted — nothing snaps, nothing rushes. It feels handmade, sacred, and quietly devastating.

The emotional core, in one breath: **a man turned his love into a living rose, and gave the woman he loves the power to keep it blooming — one day, one return, one act of care at a time.**

---

*For the video prompts: lean into the fairytale-under-glass imagery, the rose as the relationship, the daily ritual of returning, petals falling vs. blooming, a world of stars and gardens growing from devotion, and the Beauty-and-the-Beast enchantment — but reframed as hope instead of a curse. The audience feeling you're going for is: "I want to make something like this for someone" / "this is what love should feel like."*
